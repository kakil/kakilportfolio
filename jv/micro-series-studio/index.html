Affiliate FAQ

Questions your audience may ask

Use these answers directly or adapt them without changing the underlying facts.

What exactly is Micro-Series Studio?

A browser-based production workflow for planning and organizing serialized short-form stories. It connects story architecture, character canon, episode planning, episode production, visual guidance, publishing assets, backup/export, and final source-clip assembly.

Does it generate AI videos?

No. Buyers use compatible external image/video tools to create source media. MSS supplies the organized planning, prompts, continuity rules, review state, provider guidance, and browser-based Episode Merger.

Do buyers need their own AI account?

Yes. The Core workflow is designed to work with compatible external AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or equivalent tools. External providers may have their own fees, credits, or usage limits.

Is there a monthly Micro-Series Studio fee?

No. Core is currently $27 one-time. The optional Pro OTO is $47 one-time. External services are separate.

50% on Core and 50% on the Pro OTO. At current prices that is $13.50 gross on a Core sale and $23.50 gross on a Pro sale, subject to refunds, reversals, platform adjustments, and applicable fees.

Who is the ideal buyer?

AI content creators, micro-drama and serialized-fiction creators, short-form storytellers, faceless-channel builders, AI-video experimenters, and marketers who want a more structured workflow around the tools they already use.

What is the main promotional angle?

Continuity and production organization. A general AI chat can generate content, but maintaining characters, story state, episode logic, visual decisions, and publishing assets across a series becomes difficult. MSS gives that production process one home.
